## DEFECT: POST /art_manage/meta always returns 403

**Root cause**: Forms on /art_manage do NOT contain `<input type="hidden" name="csrf_token" ...>` fields. The server-side CSRF check on POST /art_manage/meta correctly validates the csrf_token parameter and rejects requests without it (returns 403 "Доступ запрещён"). But since the HTML forms don't include the csrf_token input, users cannot submit the forms from the browser — the feature is broken.

**Impact**: Users cannot edit article metadata through the management interface. This is a HIGH severity defect.
